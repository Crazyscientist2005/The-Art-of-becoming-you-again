About the Writer

Manjil Mahata

Student · Founder of Zevrix Labs · Aspiring Author

Manjil Mahata is a student and the founder of Zevrix Labs. Alongside his studies and work, he developed a deep passion for writing — driven by a genuine desire to make a difference in the lives of those around him.

This book was written especially for his juniors and fellow classmates — so that they could find their own path, believe in their potential, and never give up no matter how hard the journey gets.

Manjil believes that growth is not a destination — it is a daily choice. This book is his way of sharing that message with the world.
