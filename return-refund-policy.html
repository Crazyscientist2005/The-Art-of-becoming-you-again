Customer Policies

Shipping, Cancellation & Refund Policy

Shipping Policy

Physical Book

  • Orders will be processed after payment confirmation.
  • Shipping begins once printing is completed.
  • Delivery times depend on your location and courier service.

E-Book

  • The e-book will be available instantly after purchase once officially released.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

E-Book Orders

All e-book purchases are non-refundable and cannot be cancelled after payment has been completed.

Because digital products are delivered electronically, no refunds or exchanges will be provided.

Physical Book Orders

Customers may request cancellation within 5 days of placing the order, provided the book has not already been shipped.

If the order has already been dispatched, cancellation will no longer be possible.

💳 Approved refunds for eligible cancellations will be processed using the original payment method.
